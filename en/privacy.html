Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Bible Anywhere is a Bible app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It collects as little as it can: no account, and nothing that identifies you.

What we collect

Bible Anywhere has no account. To fix crashes and see which features are used, the apps on Mac, iPhone and iPad send two kinds of data to Google Firebase, which processes it for the developer: crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics: where the app crashed, device model, system and app version, and an installation ID) and usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase: screens and features used, app version, device type, country or approximate region from the IP address, and an app instance ID). This data does not include your name, email, Bible marks or anything you type, is not linked to who you are, and is not used for advertising or to track you across apps; the advertising identifier is not used. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

On iPhone and iPad only, the free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad SDK collects device and advertising data, an approximate location from the IP address, and diagnostics to show and measure ads. The app does not ask to track you, so your advertising identifier is not used. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first and can change it later in Settings ▸ Ad Privacy Settings. Buying Pro removes the ads. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

The Mac app shows no ads; it sends only the crash reports and usage statistics described above.

What stays on your device

Your settings, reading position and shelf are stored in the app's own folder on your device. Highlights, notes and bookmarks are stored there too and, while iCloud sync is on (Settings ▸ iCloud), also in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account get them. That database belongs to your Apple Account: the developer cannot read it. Turn sync off and everything stays on the device only. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that device; the iCloud copy can be removed in your device's iCloud settings.

Network

The Bible texts and cross references are inside the app, so reading, searching and read aloud work offline. The network is used for iCloud sync of your own marks (when it is on), crash reports and usage statistics, and, on iPhone and iPad, the ad banner.

Purchases

Bible Anywhere Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Pro is unlocked. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

The app does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information; crash reports and usage statistics are not linked to who you are.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: archivelabapps@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Bible Anywhere apps and this website. The website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources.

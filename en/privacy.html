Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Verse Anywhere is a Bible app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It is built to work without collecting any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Verse Anywhere has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising. The developer receives no data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your settings, reading position, shelf, highlights, notes and bookmarks are stored in the app's own folder on your device. They never leave it unless you copy, share, drag or export them yourself. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network

The Bible texts and cross references are inside the app, so reading, searching and read aloud work offline. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Verse Anywhere Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether Pro is unlocked. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: archivelabapps@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Verse Anywhere apps and this website. The website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources.

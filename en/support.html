Frequently asked questions

The Quick Find shortcut does nothing on my Mac.

Another shortcut may already use it. If macOS uses it (for example ⌃⌥Space switches input sources), Settings shows a warning; choose a different shortcut in Bible Anywhere ▸ Settings ▸ General. Another app's shortcut cannot be detected, so try a different one if Quick Find still does not open.

How do I restore Pro on a new Mac?

Open Settings ▸ General and click Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. With Family Sharing, family members get Pro too.

Which features are free?

On the Mac: reading, search, two translations, cross references, highlights, notes, bookmarks, read aloud, themes, copying from the reader and verse links. Pro adds Quick Find copying, the Services menu and Markdown export. On iPhone and iPad every feature is free with ads, and Pro removes the ads.

Is the iPhone and iPad version really free?

Yes. Every feature is free with a small banner below the text. Pro (one purchase) removes the ads; if you bought Pro on the Mac, tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

Do my highlights and notes sync between devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud: sign in with the same Apple Account on your Mac, iPhone and iPad and keep Settings ▸ iCloud sync on. If the same verse is changed on two devices before they sync, the later change wins; two different notes are both kept.

Why is NIV, ESV or 개역개정 not included?

Those translations are copyrighted and need a license from their publishers. Bible Anywhere includes only public-domain texts.

How do I type references?

English or Korean book names and common abbreviations all work: “John 3:16”, “Rom 8:28-39”, “Ps 23”, “요 3:16”, “고전 13:4~7”. Separate several with semicolons.

Does it need the internet?

No. All texts, cross references and the read-aloud voices are on your device.

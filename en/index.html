Three ways it saves you time

  1. Quick Find, from any app

    Type one reference or several (“Ps 23; 1 Cor 13:4-7”), or a few words. Return copies them in the style you chose.

  2. A calm reader, two translations

    Read two translations side by side, open cross references next to the text, and come back to the verse you left.

  3. Collect while you prepare

    Gather verses for a sermon, lesson or study on the shelf, drag a card into your document, or copy them all at once.

Made for people who write with Scripture

  • Copy the way you write

    Reference after or before the text, a Markdown quote, an Obsidian callout, the reference in parentheses, or one verse per line for slides.

  • Highlights, notes, bookmarks

    Five highlight colours, a note on any verse, bookmarks for the places you return to. Notes belong to the verse, so they show in every translation.

  • Read aloud

    The chapter read with the voices built into your device, offline, with the verse being read marked and followed. It goes on into the next chapter.

  • Notes and manuscripts

    Copy Link reopens the passage from Notes, Pages or Obsidian. Select a sermon outline and the Services menu puts each line's verses right under it.

  • Cross references

    118,000 links from OpenBible.info. Select a verse to see related passages, preview them and jump with one tap.

  • Sepia, Dark and Myeongjo

    Reading themes for long sessions and a Myeongjo or old-style serif face, with every text size you need.

  • Keyboard first on the Mac

    ⌘F to search, ↑↓ to move between verses, ←→ to change chapters, ⌘L to go to a reference. Every command is in the menu.

  • Private and offline

    No account, no ads, no tracking. Every text is on your device and works without a connection.
